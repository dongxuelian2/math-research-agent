## Recommended next attack order

R-01 and R-02 are first because they can convert otherwise valid-looking provider evidence into semantic authority. R-03 and R-04 follow because projection disagreement or cross-store recovery can corrupt terminal truth. R-05/R-06 then attack root completeness and verifier independence. R-07/R-08/R-09 address reconstruction, source authority, and evidence provenance. R-10/R-11 close the platform and late-result consumer surfaces.
